# Kabocha Soup

## Ingredients
- Kombu
- Katsuobushi
- Miso
- Ginger
- Kabocha
- [Coconut cream]
- [Pine nuts]
- [Cilantro]

## Instructions
1. Cut and de-seed kabocha
2. Roast kabocha at 400°F for 30m
3. Steep kombu and katsuobushi in a low simmer for 10m
4. Crush and bloom ginger
5. Add strained dashi and miso
6. De-skin kabocha and add to broth
7. Bring to a simmer and immersion blend
8. Add soy sauce, salt, MSG to taste
9. Optionally add coconut cream
10. Garnish with toasted pine nuts and cilantro
